# Career Progression Timeline

A vertical milestone tracker created for the Experience page (`main/index2.html`) to showcase career promotions and role progression at an organization.

## Anatomy
- **Header**: Company name in narrow display typography + pill badge `Career Progression`.
- **Active Node**: Yellow square node (`#ffe32e`, border-radius 4px) containing a black star symbol (`✦`).
- **Gradient Stem**: Vertical connector line flowing from the active node down to past milestones.
- **Past Node**: Inactive dark disc (`●`).
- **Role Body**: Title, status pill (`PROMOTED ✦` or `INITIAL ROLE`), and monospace uppercase date span.
