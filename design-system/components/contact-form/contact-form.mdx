# Contact Form

The retro-styled inline communication form used in the black `.plan` section of `main/index.html` and `main/index3.html`. Incorporates custom monospace inputs (`E-MAIL`, `MESSAGE`), the yellow pill `ENTER` button, and the synchronized 4-box spinning windmill loader on the right.

## Anatomy
- **Input Fields**: Monospace font, uppercase placeholders, yellow focus ring.
- **Submit Wrap**: Relative anchor grouping the submit button and floating spinner.
- **Loader**: 4 offset spinning boxes with `SENDING...` status text.
- **Feedback Message**: Inline success/error status below the form.
